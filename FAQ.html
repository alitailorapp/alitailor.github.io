Frequently Asked Questions

Ali Tailor — quick, clear answers

Everything you need to know about using Ali Tailor. Tap a question to see the answer.

1. Is Ali Tailor free to use?

Ali Tailor is free to download and use with ads. A Premium subscription (monthly, quarterly, half-yearly or yearly) removes all ads and lifts the PDF limit. Measurement packages are also available as one-time purchases.

2. Does the app work offline?

Yes. Customer records, orders, measurements, credits and images are stored locally on your device, so the app works offline. An internet connection is only needed for Google Drive backup and restore, and for displaying ads.

3. Where is my data stored? Is it safe?

Your business data stays on your device in a secure Room database. When you sign in with Google, only your name and email are stored in Firebase for sign-in. Nothing else is uploaded unless you choose to back up to Google Drive.

4. How do I back up and restore my data?

Use the backup option to save your data to your own Google Drive account. Included: customer measurements, credit list, orders (Pending, Working, Completed), registration form edits and app settings. Not included: delivery orders and credit collections.

5. Can I create and share PDFs?

Yes — you can create PDFs for measurements, orders and credits. Free users can watch an optional rewarded ad to unlock saving or sharing. Premium subscribers have no limit and see no ads.

6. Which languages does the app support?

Ali Tailor supports seven languages: Urdu, English, Hindi, Bengali, Nepali, Arabic and Farsi.

7. How can I delete my account or data?

You can delete individual customers, orders, credits and collections inside the app. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data. To delete the name and email stored in Firebase, email alitailorapp@gmail.com.

8. How do subscriptions and refunds work?

All payments are processed by Google Play Store and prices may vary by region. If you have an issue with a purchase, email alitailorapp@gmail.com within 3 days. While Premium is active, ads are removed; if it expires or is cancelled, ads return automatically.
